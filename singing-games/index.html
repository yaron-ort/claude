Singing games

Train your ear and your voice. Pick a game.

Pitch GliderSteer a glider with your voice. Sing high to fly up, low to go down, and hit the gates.
Pitch Glider: By earEvery gate is in the middle. Listen to the note and find it with your voice.
Name that NoteHear a note and pick it from a ladder of choices laid out by height, like E2 or C3. No mic needed.
Song Notes NewPlay your own songs and see which note the singer is on right now, like E3 or A3.
Walk and SingSound only, for walks: Sing along, Simon, Echo and more. Headphones on, screen off.
Pitch PalLoad a song and sing along while a graph shows your voice against the melody.
Tip: games that use the mic work best with headphones.
