Walk and Sing

No screen needed. The phone sings a long note, then you sing it back and hold it. While you sing you hear live sounds: high beeps mean go up, low beeps mean go down, and they come faster the farther off you are. Climbing chimes mean you're on the note, so keep holding. Then it moves on to the next note.

Bluetooth headphones: connect them after the mic is on.
