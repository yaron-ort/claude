Walk and Sing

No screen needed. The phone sings a long note, then you sing it back and hold it. While you sing you hear live sounds: high beeps mean go up, low beeps mean go down. One beep: just a little. Two beeps: about one step. Three beeps: far. Climbing chimes mean you're on the note, so keep holding. Then it moves on to the next note.

Bluetooth headphones: connect them after the mic is on.
